# Claude Chrome extension prompts: proposals with resume

Before use: resume PDF saved in Downloads, logged in to Upwork in Chrome.
The extension cannot use the computer's file picker, so it stops at "Attach files" and Adam picks the resume himself.

## Prompt 1: attach resume to an already submitted proposal
Go to upwork.com (I am already logged in). Open Find Work, then Proposals, and find my submitted proposal for the job "[JOB TITLE]". Open it and check whether Upwork lets me add an attachment to it (look for Edit, Change terms or an attachments section).
If it does: click to attach a file, then STOP and tell me to choose "Adam-Owolabi-WordPress-Developer-Resume.pdf" from my Downloads folder. After I choose it, save the change. Do not change the bid, milestones or cover letter.
If Upwork does not allow attachments on a submitted proposal, do not withdraw or resend anything. Just tell me so.

## Prompt 2: apply to a job with resume attached (Claude fills the brackets)
Open this Upwork job: [PASTE JOB LINK]. Click "Apply now".
1. Do NOT boost the proposal. Leave any boost or "bid for top spot" field at zero.
2. Set the bid to exactly: [AMOUNT]. If it is a fixed-price job with milestones, enter these milestones exactly: [MILESTONES].
3. Paste this cover letter exactly as written, with no changes:
[COVER LETTER]
4. Answer any screening questions with these answers: [ANSWERS]. If there is a question I did not give an answer for, STOP and ask me.
5. Click "Attach files", then STOP and tell me to choose "Adam-Owolabi-WordPress-Developer-Resume.pdf" from my Downloads folder.
6. Show me the full proposal page before submitting and wait for me to say "submit". Never submit without my OK.
Never share an email, phone number or any contact outside Upwork, and never offer a call.
