# Proposal Reference Sites

Use these as **design references** in proposals: "the kind of site I will build for you". Never write "I built" or "my work" about them. Your own work is only what is in the Upwork portfolio.
Features below were detected on each homepage on 2026-11-14.

## How to use one in a proposal (copy and adapt)

> Looking at the top [NICHE] websites in the US, the ones that win calls do 3 things above the fold: [FEATURE 1], [FEATURE 2] and [FEATURE 3]. A good example is [SITE]. Your site has [WEAK SPOT YOU FOUND]. I would rebuild your homepage in Elementor around that same pattern, keep your branding, and make it load fast on phones.

Pick 1 reference per proposal, the one closest to the client's niche. Always pair it with something specific you found on the client's own site.

## Example: roofing job

> Hi [Name], I looked at your site. It takes about 8 seconds to load on a phone and the quote button only appears at the bottom of the page. The roofing sites that win the most calls, like tadlockroofing.com, put a free inspection button, click to call and their reviews right at the top, and they load fast on mobile. I would rebuild your homepage in Elementor around that same pattern, keep your branding, and get it loading in under 3 seconds. I can start this week. Would a fixed price of $X with a 5 day timeline work for you?
