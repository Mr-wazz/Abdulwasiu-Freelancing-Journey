# Proposal Reply System

Goal: replies, interviews, direct offers. Not just "proposal sent".

## 1. Be early
Most hires happen among the first proposals. Apply within 1 to 3 hours of posting. That is why the scan runs every hour.

## 2. The first 2 lines decide everything
The client sees only the first ~200 characters in their list. Never open with "Hi, I am a WordPress developer with X years".
Open with THEIR site and a real number:
> "Your homepage takes 7.9s to load on a phone and the booking button is below the fold, so most mobile visitors leave before they see it. I can fix both this week."

## 3. Follow their instructions exactly
If they ask for a start word, a question answered, or 2 links: do it in the first lines. Many proposals get ignored for skipping this.

## 4. Short and specific (120 to 180 words)
- Line 1 to 2: their problem with a real number from their site.
- Line 3 to 5: the plan in 3 steps.
- One relevant portfolio link (the closest niche), not a list.
- One niche reference from proposal-reference-sites.md as "what works", never as own work.
- Price and timeline, split into phases (small phase 1).

## 5. Give value before they reply
Attach a free 1-page mini audit of their site (phone speed, mobile screenshot, 3 issues, fix plan). Almost nobody does this. It turns "another proposal" into "this person already started".

## 6. End with an easy question
> "Should I start with a no-login speed diagnosis, or would you prefer to send temporary admin access?"
A yes/no question gets more replies than "Looking forward to hearing from you".

## 7. Price to get the first yes, then grow
Phase 1 small and clear (audit or first fix). The $5K comes from the Growth Rebuild and the monthly plan after delivery.

## 8. Reply fast when they answer
Upwork app on the phone with notifications on. A reply within 10 minutes often wins the contract.

## 9. Direct offers and invites (no Connects)
- Keep the 3 catalog offers active and priced right.
- Every finished job: ask for a 5 star review. Reviews and the Rising Talent badge bring invites.
- Keep availability "More than 30 hrs/week".

## 10. Track and improve
Log every proposal in the pipeline: job, opening line used, reply yes/no. Every week keep the openings that got replies and drop the rest.
