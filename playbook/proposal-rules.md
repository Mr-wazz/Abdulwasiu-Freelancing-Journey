# Proposal Rules (set by Adam, 2026-11-14)

1. Only jobs with 25 or fewer proposals already sent (Adam raised it from 10 to 25 on 2026-11-21).
2. Connects limit: up to 10 Connects per proposal. Up to 20 Connects only when the client has spent $10K+ on Upwork (Adam approved 2026-11-14).
3. Only jobs that match what Adam does: WordPress, Elementor, WooCommerce, speed, bug fixes, maintenance, redesigns. Never apply to anything else (no Webflow, no GoHighLevel, no Shopify, no design-only, no marketing or VA roles).
4. Remote jobs only. Never on-site or office jobs.
5. Location: Adam confirmed on 2026-11-14 that he is in the United States. Apply to US-only remote jobs and worldwide remote jobs.
6. Use reference sites from proposal-reference-sites.md as design examples only, never as Adam's own work.
7. Every proposal opens with a real finding from the client's own site (speed, mobile, missing tracking, missing call to action).
8. Auto-send: Adam gave standing approval on 2026-11-20. Proposals that pass every rule are submitted without waiting, with relevant portfolio projects attached, no boost, no files. Only Adam's own portfolio sites are cited.

9. Payment verified clients only. One exception (Adam, 2026-11-23): a client who is brand new to Upwork (no jobs posted before, no hires yet) with an unverified payment method MAY be applied to only when the job post is very detailed and reads like a serious buyer (clear scope, real business, site or examples named, sensible budget). Rule 11 (hire rate 50%+) does not apply to these brand-new clients. Flag every such submission to Adam.
10. Never apply to clients located in Nigeria, India, Pakistan or Bangladesh (Adam's rule, 2026-11-15).
11. Never apply when the client's hire rate is below 50% (Adam's rule, 2026-11-21). This also excludes brand-new clients with no hires yet.
12. Hourly bid: $25/hr (Adam changed his rate from $35 to $25 on 2026-11-21). Fixed-price offers stay priced by value.
13. No calls (Adam's rule, 2026-11-21). Adam works by live chat on Upwork. Proposals and replies offer live chat, never a call or video meeting. Each proposal has one live chat line, then closes with an either/or question about the result (see proposal-structure.md, updated 2026-11-23).
14. Price, discount, deadline or contract questions in messages: never commit. Reply "Give me a minute to recheck our messages and get back to you." and alert Adam at once.

15. Proposal format: every proposal follows playbook/proposal-structure.md (Adam's framework, adopted 2026-11-23). It replaces the old 120 to 180 word format.

16. Every proposal (Adam, 2026-11-23): follows proposal-structure.md, includes the portfolio link https://adam-owolabi.netlify.app in the sign-off, and has the resume PDF attached (resume/Adam-Owolabi-WordPress-Developer-Resume.pdf).

17. Boosting (Adam, 2026-11-23, replaces "no boost"): never bid more than 16 Connects on a boost. Aim for 2nd or 3rd place in the top 4. Read the current top bids from the proposal preview and bid 1 Connect above the place you want:
    - 2nd place = (current 2nd bid + 1), if that is 16 or less.
    - Otherwise 3rd place = (current 3rd bid + 1), if that is 16 or less.
    - If neither fits under 16, do not boost and tell Adam.
    - Example: bids 23, 14, 13, 5, 2. 2nd place costs 15, which is under 16, so bid 15.
18. Learn from the numbers (Adam, 2026-11-23): track every proposal in playbook/proposal-stats.md (viewed, replied, interview, hired, Connects spent, boost bid, proposals at send, hours after posting) and review it before writing new proposals. Change the framework when the numbers show what works.
