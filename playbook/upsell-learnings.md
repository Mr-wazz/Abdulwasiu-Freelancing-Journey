### 12 Nov 2026: tools for network marketing team leaders (for Motion)
From one web search of software listing sites. Prices on those sites can be out of date, so check the vendor page before quoting.
- Company wide MLM back office software is listed from about 995 to 1,599 USD a month (MLMTrees, Flawless Core, Lead MLM Software, ProMLM).
- Apps for a single recruiter are listed at about 5 to 7 USD a month (Team Build Pro), or almost free (Pamtree).
- No well known product was found between 10 and 100 USD a month for a leader who wants to track their own team. That is the opening for the Motion portal.
- Sources: capterra.com/p/188893/Pamtree, softwareadvice.com/product/539047-MLMTrees, getapp.com/all-software/a/flawless-core, capterra.com/p/175021/Lead-MLM-Software, getapp.com/mlm-software/a/pro-mlm, apps.apple.com/us/app/id6751211622

## Learnings from Amazigh (RiseTrack), 14 Oct 2026
- Pattern: approval jobs are a door opener. Remove risky features for v1 on purpose (fake Premium), keep the real version as the paid step 2.
- Pricing plan (not yet tested): 6 packages totaling 5,000, first sale packages 1 and 3 (2,100). Result: pending.
- Budget careful client: shrink scope, do not discount per feature. Fixed price, milestones, funded escrow.
- Hide prices at first, lead with value.
- Offer via a short value message in the client's language (French).
- Risk: client offers off platform payment (Sendwave). Keep contracts on platform where possible.

## Learnings from Amazigh (RiseTrack), 25 Oct 2026
- Client screen recordings (WhatsApp video) are the best bug evidence for wrapped web apps. Extract frames with a small Swift AVFoundation script when ffmpeg is missing.
- Wrapped web app trap: a permanent CSS transform on a scroll container breaks every position fixed element (floating buttons, dialogs) inside the iOS WebView. Test create, edit and delete flows after every layout change.
- Price page, locks and native code can be built early and hidden behind a flag, then switched on after Apple approves.
- Client pays at the end and keeps adding polish requests. Include small bug fixes, quote new features, keep a visible list.

## Learnings from Motion (own team portal), 12 Nov 2026
- Own tools can become products. Build the first version for yourself, use it for a month, then sell it with your own results as the proof.
- Asking many questions with a suggested answer for each got fast replies. He only answered the ones he cared about and accepted the rest.
- Voice style messages get cut off. Repeat back what was understood before building.
- Pricing idea, not yet tested: 15 USD a month for up to 30 members, 39 USD for up to 100, plus a 100 USD setup service.
