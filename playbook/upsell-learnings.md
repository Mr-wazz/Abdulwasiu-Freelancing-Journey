## Learnings from Amazigh (RiseTrack), 14 Oct 2026
- Pattern: approval jobs are a door opener. Remove risky features for v1 on purpose (fake Premium), keep the real version as the paid step 2.
- Pricing plan (not yet tested): 6 packages totaling 5,000, first sale packages 1 and 3 (2,100). Result: pending.
- Budget careful client: shrink scope, do not discount per feature. Fixed price, milestones, funded escrow.
- Hide prices at first, lead with value.
- Offer via a short value message in the client's language (French).
- Risk: client offers off platform payment (Sendwave). Keep contracts on platform where possible.
