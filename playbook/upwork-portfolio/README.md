## How to add each one on Upwork (5 minutes each)
1. Go to upwork.com, click your photo (top right), then **View profile**.
2. Scroll to **Portfolio** and click the **+** button.
3. **Project title:** paste the title below.
4. **Your role:** paste the role.
5. **Project description:** paste the description.
6. **Skills and deliverables:** type each skill below and pick it from the list.
7. **Content:** click **Image**, upload the matching PNG from this folder. Then click **Link** and paste the live URL.
8. Click **Publish**. Repeat for the next one.
Add them in this order: the first one shows first on your profile.
