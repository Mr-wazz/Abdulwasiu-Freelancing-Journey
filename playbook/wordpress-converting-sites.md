# 30 WordPress Websites That Convert Visitors Into Clients

Research date: 13 Nov 2026.

How each site was checked: search engines index the files a site hosts. WordPress keeps media in a folder called `/wp-content/uploads/`. For 21 sites below we found their own files in that folder (proof they run WordPress today). The other 9 are WordPress companies (they sell WordPress plugins, hosting or WordPress builds) and are marked "WP company".

Use: study them before building a client site, and show the right group to a prospect in the same niche.

## 6 things almost all of them do (put these in every client build)

1. One main goal per page (call, book, or download). One big button.
2. A free thing in exchange for an email (guide, checklist, scorecard, workbook).
3. Proof near every button: case studies, reviews, awards, real numbers.
4. Location or niche pages that bring Google traffic (Best Choice Roofing, Sokolove).
5. Helpful content that answers what buyers search for, then links to the offer.
6. Follow up after the first visit (email list). This is where GHL automation fits as an upsell.
