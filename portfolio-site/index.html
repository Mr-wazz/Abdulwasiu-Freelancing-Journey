FAQ

Questions clients ask.

How do we work together?

Everything runs through Upwork, so your funds are protected and every message is in one place. I work over live chat on Upwork, which keeps every decision in writing.

How much does a project cost?

Every job gets a fixed price or a clear hourly estimate before I start. Bigger projects are split into milestones, so you only fund one step at a time.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?

Yes. I build with Elementor and clean WordPress settings, and you get a short video walkthrough at handover.

Do I keep ownership of everything?

Always. Your hosting, domain, Google Analytics, Search Console and Tag Manager stay in your name. I work with access you give and can remove.

Can you fix a site someone else built?

Yes. Most of my fix and speed work is on sites built by other developers. I take a full backup first, then find the cause before changing anything.

What do you need from me to start?

Your website link and a short note on what is wrong or what you want. For builds: your logo, text and photos, or I can suggest a structure from top sites in your industry.
