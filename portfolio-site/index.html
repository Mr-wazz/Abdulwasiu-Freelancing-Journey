FAQ

Before you hire me.

How much will my project cost?

It depends on what your site needs, so I quote after looking at it. You always get a fixed price or a clear hourly estimate before work starts, and bigger projects are split into steps, so you only fund one step at a time.

How fast can you start?

Usually within a day. Small fixes are often done in 24 to 72 hours.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

Yes. I build with Elementor and clean WordPress settings, and you get a short video showing how to change text, photos and prices.

Do I keep ownership of everything?

Always. Your hosting, domain, Google Analytics, Search Console and Tag Manager stay in your name. I only use access you give me, and you can remove it any time.

Can you fix a site another developer built?

Yes, most fix and speed jobs are on sites built by someone else. I take a full backup first and find the real cause before changing anything.

How do we work together?

Everything runs through Upwork, so your funds are protected until you approve the work. We talk over live chat on Upwork, so every decision is in writing and easy to find later.

What do you need from me to start?

Your website link and a short note on what is wrong or what you want. For a new site: your logo, text and photos. If you do not have text yet, I can suggest a page structure based on the best sites in your industry.
