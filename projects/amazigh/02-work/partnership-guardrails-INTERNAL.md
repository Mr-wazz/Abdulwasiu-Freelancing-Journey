# Partnership option for Amazigh (INTERNAL, do not send)

## What he said (19 Oct 2026, translated)
"Brother, honestly the sums are astronomical and I am not ready to put that much money in. As I explained, it is my first project and I prefer not to invest this much without knowing if the app will generate revenue. Either I go little by little and find solutions myself, or if you accept to move forward with me, you will be paid significant sums once the app starts earning money."

## How to read it
- Not a no. He gave two paths and the second one means he wants YOU as a long term partner.
- His real fear: spending before he knows the app earns. His budget number is still unknown.
- Risk for you: "paid later" with no cash, no contract and no escrow can end as unpaid work. Do not accept a pure promise.

## Safe version: hybrid partnership
1. Cash first for the first small win (step A, or at least A1 at 100 CAD). No work before the first payment.
2. Defer only part of the price, never the whole job. Rule: no more than about 50 percent of any package deferred.
3. Deferred part carries a risk premium (suggestion: +30 percent) and is repaid from a percentage of NET subscription revenue (after Apple's commission), suggestion 25 percent, paid monthly with a statement.
4. After the deferred part is repaid: a smaller ongoing share (suggestion 10 percent) for 12 months, or the care plan instead. This is your long run income.
5. Cap and duration written down (example: total never above 2 times the deferred amount, maximum 24 months).
6. Transparency: read only access to RevenueCat (and App Store Connect sales) so you can both see the numbers.
7. He owns the app, code and accounts. You never hold his accounts hostage. If he stops, he keeps everything delivered.
8. Stop loss: if after 6 months of the subscription being live there is almost no revenue, the deferred balance stays open but you stop new unpaid work.
9. Everything in writing in the chat (and a one page agreement) before work starts.

## Example (illustrative, Revenus package 1,600 CAD)
- Upfront cash: 600 (step A 430 + deletion 100 + logo 70).
- Deferred: 1,000 (subscription base) plus 30 percent premium = 1,300, repaid at 25 percent of net subscription revenue.
- Months to repay 1,300: net revenue 150 per month = about 35 months. 500 per month = about 10 months. 1,500 per month = about 3.5 months.
- Lesson: a revenue share only works if the app really earns. So keep real cash for the base, defer little, and ask for his user count and subscription price before agreeing.
