# Behance Proposal Template

Rules: under 150 words. First line is about THEIR goal, never "Hi, I am a designer". One specific detail from their post. One proof. A simple plan. End with a question.

## Template
Hi [Name],

[One line that repeats their goal in money or result terms. Example: "You want a brand and site that make [business] look premium enough to win bigger clients."]

[One specific observation from their post or current site. Example: "I noticed your current site has no booking button on mobile, so visitors have to hunt for how to reach you."]

I do both the design and the build, so you get one person from first sketch to live launch. Recent example: [matching proof, for example "I took a habit app from 2 Apple rejections to App Store approval, with a new icon and store screenshots."]

My plan:
1. [Step 1, for example short call and moodboard]
2. [Step 2, for example design of the key screens or pages]
3. [Step 3, for example build, test, launch]

I can share 2 relevant samples and a fixed price after one quick question: [smart question, for example "is the main goal more bookings or a more premium look?"]
