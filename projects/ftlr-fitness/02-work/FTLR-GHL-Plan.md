# FTLR Fitness: Growth System Plan (GoHighLevel)

Prepared by Up Agency for Leah Stukov, For the Long Run Fitness. November 2026.

## The goal
Get FTLR to 1,000 users, and keep them training (and paying) once they join.
The app stays exactly as it is. GoHighLevel sits beside it and handles the follow up that is hard to do by hand.

## What you already have to build on
- A live iPhone app with Monthly and Annual plans
- Founding members on the web app
- Your 45 minute training session offer for members who move to the App Store
- Your Instagram audience

## Phase 1: Quick wins (weeks 1 and 2)
1. **Booking calendar for your 45 minute sessions.** Members pick a time that suits you, and get email and text reminders. No back and forth.
2. **Founding member move to the App Store.** One simple page: "Move my membership" form, then straight to booking the free session. Every request is tracked, so nobody is forgotten.
3. **7 day welcome series for new app members.** Short emails (and one or two texts) from you: welcome, first workout, meet your coach, recipe of the week, how to log progress, and an invite to rate the app.
4. **Your contact list in one place.** Your current members imported, tagged as founding members, web members or app members.

What you will see: sessions booked without chasing, smoother moves to the app, new members who actually start training.

## Phase 2: Keep members (weeks 3 to 6)
5. **"We miss you" messages.** Gentle, automatic check ins for members who go quiet, before they cancel.
6. **Referral campaign.** Founding members invite friends with a personal link. You see who brought who, and can thank or reward them.
7. **App review requests.** Happy members are asked for an App Store rating, which helps new people find FTLR.

What you will see: fewer cancellations, more ratings, new members from people who already love FTLR.

## Phase 3: Grow (weeks 6 to 10)
8. **Free challenge landing page** (for example, a free 7 day "For the Long Run" starter plan) to collect new leads from Instagram.
9. **Instagram follow up.** People who comment or message a keyword get the link and join your list automatically.
10. **Monthly newsletter template** that you can send in minutes.
11. **Simple dashboard.** New leads, bookings, members and where they came from, on one screen.

What you will see: a steady flow of new people from Instagram into the app, and a clear view of what works.
