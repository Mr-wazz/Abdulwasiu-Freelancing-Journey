ABOUT THE APP
FTLR Fitness is a fitness coaching app (workouts, programs, recipes, coach chat) by For the Long Run Fitness LLC.

HOW IT WORKS
1. The app opens on a native subscription screen with two auto-renewable subscriptions: Monthly and Yearly. They are bought with Apple In-App Purchase. Restore Purchases, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy are on the same screen.
2. To test a purchase: tap a plan, then Subscribe, and confirm with your sandbox Apple Account. After the purchase the app unlocks (this can take up to 15 seconds, please wait on the "Please wait" button), then opens the member area.
3. EXISTING MEMBERS: tap "Already a member? Log in to your account" and sign in with the demo account in the Sign-in fields. Sign in is email and password only. The app checks that the account has an active membership, so no new purchase is needed for a verified member.
4. No payment method other than Apple In-App Purchase is offered inside the app. There are no external purchase links or prices for outside payments.
