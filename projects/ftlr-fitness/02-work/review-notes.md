3. WHAT THE APP DOES AND WHO IT IS FOR
FTLR Fitness is a coaching app from For the Long Run Fitness LLC (coach Leah Stukov). It gives members workouts and programs, recipes that fit dietary preferences, coach Q&A and a member community. Audience: adults who want guided strength, power and mobility training that fits real life. It solves the problem of having no structured, coach led plan, and gives members one place to follow workouts and recipes.

4. HOW TO ACCESS THE APP
Option A, existing member: on the first screen tap "Already a member? Log in to your account", then sign in with the demo account in the Sign-in fields (email and password only; there is no Google or Apple login). The account has an active membership, so all features open.
Option B, new purchase: tap a plan, tap Subscribe and confirm with a sandbox Apple Account. After the purchase, wait up to 15 seconds on the "Please wait" button, then the member area opens. Restore Purchases is on the same screen.
Main features: Home, Workouts, Recipes, Plan, Ask Coach, Chat, Resources.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit) for payments in the app; RevenueCat for subscription status; Base44 for hosting, login (email and password) and member data. The iPhone app offers no other payment method and shows no external purchase links or outside prices. Members who bought on the coach's website (billed on the web, not through the app) can log in with their account. Apple purchase is also offered.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app and its content are the same in every region. Content is in English. Prices are shown by Apple in the local currency.

7. REGULATED INDUSTRY
Not a regulated industry. All workouts, videos, text and branding are owned by For the Long Run Fitness LLC. A health and safety disclaimer is in the Terms of Use.
