# Screen recording for Apple (record on your own physical iPhone, latest iOS)

How: Control Center, tap Screen Recording (start). Stop from the red bar. Keep it 2 to 4 minutes. Do not show passwords: type them before recording, or show dots only.
Start from the Home Screen so the recording begins with launching the app. Use a build installed from TestFlight (build 120).

Order to show:
1. Tap the FTLR Fitness icon (launch).
2. Subscription screen: scroll to show titles, prices, renewal text, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links.
3. Tap Subscribe, confirm with the sandbox account (the sheet says "Environment: Sandbox"), wait for the member area.
4. Member area: Home, Workouts, Recipes, Plan, Ask Coach, Chat.
5. Group chat: show how a member reports or blocks another user (if the app has this). If members cannot post, show that.
6. Account deletion: Settings, Delete Account, confirm. Use a throwaway account, never the demo account.
7. Log out. Reinstall is not needed. Show login path: first screen, "Already a member? Log in", sign in with the demo account.
8. Show Restore Purchases on the subscription screen.
9. Show that no permission pop-ups appear (camera, location, tracking).

Upload: Resolution Center, Reply to App Review, attach the video file (or a link). If the file is too large, shorten it or compress it.
