# Opportunity research: High Achievement Center (30 Oct 2026)

Currency: USD. Prices and revenue numbers are my estimates unless a source is named. Confirm on the Zoom.

## What the site has today (highachievementcenter.com)
- Offers: Elite Coaching (1 on 1), Leadership & Communication, Team & Employee Coaching, Speaking (leadership, financial literacy, credit strategies, emotional intelligence, purpose).
- One call to action everywhere: Free Clarity Call on Calendly.
- Two founders: Mr. Joe (coach, speaker) and the co-founder (president, runs strategy, course development, systems).
- Claims 20+ years of experience.

## Weak spots that cost money
1. Every button goes to the same booking link. Visitors not ready to book leave. No email capture, no lead magnet.
2. No prices or packages anywhere, so visitors cannot self qualify.
3. Only 3 short testimonials, no results or case studies.
4. 760 Mindset program is named in the footer, with no page or description. It is an unsold asset.
5. Leadership page lists 4 modules but no format, length or price.
6. Menu links overlap (two tracks point to the same page).
7. No video on the site, only a YouTube link.
8. No visible follow up after a booking (reminders, no show recovery, post call email) that we can see from outside.
9. Speaking has a good inquiry form but no packages or past client list.

## Ideas, ranked by realism and speed to money
1. **Clarity call funnel in GHL (fastest win).** Booking page, SMS and email reminders, no show recovery, post call follow up, pipeline in CRM. Same traffic, more booked and closed calls. Price idea: $1,500 to $2,500 setup, $250 per month.
2. **Leadership quiz plus email list.** He already liked quizzes on the first call. Quiz sorts people into his 4 tracks, then an automatic email sequence invites them to a call. Price idea: $800 to $1,500.
3. **Productize 760 Mindset as a paid group cohort.** 6 to 8 weeks, live on Zoom, GHL memberships plus Stripe. Example math: 10 seats at $497 to $997 is $4,970 to $9,970 per cohort, 3 cohorts a year. Group programs are the fastest growing segment for coaches per the sources below. Price idea: $2,500 to $4,000 build.
4. **Corporate workshop packages.** Turn Team Coaching and Speaking into 3 clear packages (half day, full day, 8 week team program) with a Request a Proposal form and a proposal follow up automation. Rough market benchmark: group workshop around $12,000 per day, leadership programs $8k to $25k (vendor sources, treat as rough). Even one booking a quarter is large for him. Price idea: $2,000 to $3,500 page and automation.
5. **Financial literacy and credit workshop for employers and communities.** His speaking topics already include financial literacy and elite credit strategies, and he has a credit counselor background (Noomii listing). Package it as an employee benefit workshop. This is my inference, ask him on Zoom.
6. **Light website refresh as the vehicle for 1 to 5.** Pricing and packages page, video on homepage, testimonial section, FAQ, fix menu. The co-founder already asked for the first three in May.
7. **Monthly retainer.** Updates, email newsletter, reporting. Price idea: $250 to $500 per month.

## Suggested path (phases, not one big $2,000 job)
- Phase 1 (quick win): Idea 1 plus 6 light. About $2,500 to $3,500.
- Phase 2 (growth): Idea 2 plus 3. About $3,000 to $5,000.
- Phase 3 (big contracts): Idea 4 plus 5. About $2,000 to $3,500.
- Retainer from month 1. Total path can pass $5,000 and pays for itself if he gets one corporate booking.

## Questions to ask on Zoom
1. What is your income goal for 2027 and where does it come from today?
2. How many clarity calls a month, and how many become paying clients?
3. What is Elite Coaching priced at, and how many clients now?
4. What is 760 Mindset, and is anyone buying it?
5. Do you want more corporate and speaking work or more individual clients?
6. What did you want to give me? (He said "I got something for you." Listen first.)
