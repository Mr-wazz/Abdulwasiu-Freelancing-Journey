# Lead Audit Checklist (score each item, total 100)

| Check | Points | How |
|---|---|---|
| Website loads over 3 seconds on mobile | 15 | PageSpeed Insights |
| Not mobile friendly | 15 | open on phone |
| No online booking or contact form | 15 | look at the site |
| No automation (no chat, no follow up, no lead capture) | 15 | look for widgets |
| No mobile app and customers book often | 10 | search Play Store |
| Weak SEO (no title or description, no Google Business updates) | 10 | view source, Maps profile |
| Few or unanswered Google reviews | 10 | Maps profile |
| Outdated design or broken links | 10 | click around |

Score 50 or more = contact. Under 50 = skip.

For each contacted lead save: the ONE biggest problem, an estimated monthly cost of that problem, and the offer that fixes it (GHL automation, Base44 app, WordPress rebuild).
