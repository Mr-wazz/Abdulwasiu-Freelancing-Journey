# Black Friday offer (2026-11-27)

Why this works: Black Friday is a real deadline. Businesses that sell something lose the most money if their page, offer capture and follow up are not live before it. Say this honestly. Never fake scarcity.

Today is 2026-11-16. Black Friday is 11 days away. A build must start within days to be live in time.

## Three tiers (suggested prices in USD, adjust to the lead's market and size)

| Tier | What they get | Price | Build time |
|------|---------------|-------|-----------|
| Basic: Offer Page | Black Friday landing page, countdown timer, offer popup, email capture, mobile ready | 750 | 3 to 4 days |
| Standard: Offer + Follow Up | Basic plus GHL email and SMS sequence for leads and buyers, booking or WhatsApp button, abandoned lead follow up | 1,800 | 5 to 7 days |
| Premium: Full Campaign | Standard plus ad setup (Meta or Google), analytics dashboard, 30 day support, post sale follow up for repeat buyers | 3,500 | 7 to 9 days |

Rules: never underprice. Phase bigger jobs. After Black Friday, pitch the monthly retainer (Rule 2 and Rule 3 in CLAUDE.md).

## Upsells to offer after delivery
- Monthly maintenance and promo support retainer
- Christmas and New Year campaign page
- Mobile app version (Capacitor) for repeat customers
- Review collection system
- Referral deal

## Angle for emails and WhatsApp
1. Show you looked at their channel or site.
2. Name 2 or 3 real problems, each with the sales it costs.
3. One line: Black Friday is on 27 November and the fix needs to be live before then.
4. One line on the fix, PDF has the breakdown, free preview call.

## Black Friday readiness checks (add 1 to every audit)
- No dedicated offer or promo page
- No countdown or deadline on the page
- No email or SMS capture for offer buyers
- No WhatsApp or one tap contact
- No follow up for people who did not buy
- Link in bio or video description goes to a Linktree, a broken page or the wrong page
