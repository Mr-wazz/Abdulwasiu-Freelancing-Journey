# Upsells: LifeSource

Timing: when the client is happy (milestone or delivery).
Frame: "Here is what will help you earn more".
Currency: USD. Prices not yet tested.

## Offers (at least 3)

### 1. GHL 24/7 lead capture (missed call text back, instant form reply, booking)
- Problem it solves: site says "available 24/7" but after hours a family can only call or fill a form and wait. They call the next funeral home.
- Money or time saved: one saved family = $1,295 to $6,750.
- Price: $1,500 setup + inside retainer, or part of Premium tier.
- Message to send: "Families often search for help late at night. Right now, if nobody picks up, they move on. I can set up an instant text reply and online booking so every family hears from LifeSource within a minute, even at 2 am. One saved family pays for it several times over."

### 2. Pre planning nurture (emails and texts)
- Problem it solves: pre planning leads are not ready today. Without follow up they forget.
- Money or time saved: pre need contracts are paid in advance. Converting 1 extra plan a month = $3,000 to $6,750.
- Price: $1,200 setup.
- Message to send: "People who download your guide or ask about planning ahead usually need time. A gentle 30 day series (what to expect, costs, stories like Maria's) keeps LifeSource the one they choose."

### 3. Monthly care retainer
- Problem it solves: speed and SEO slowly decay as new posts, widgets and photos are added.
- Money or time saved: keeps the site fast and leads flowing, no surprise fixes.
- Price: $497 per month.
- Message to send: "To keep the gains, I can check speed and SEO every month, make small edits and send you a simple lead report."

### 4. Review collection system
- Problem it solves: funeral choices are trust driven. More Google reviews = more Maps calls.
- Price: $600 setup.
- Message to send: "After each service, a kind automatic message asks the family for a review. More reviews means more families find you on Google Maps."

### 5. Local SEO pages per county
- Problem it solves: they serve 5 counties but have no page per county (example: "Aquamation in Orange County").
- Price: $1,800 for 5 pages.
