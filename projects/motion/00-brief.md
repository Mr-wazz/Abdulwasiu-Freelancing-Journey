# Brief: Motion (personal project)

## Client
- Business: Motion, Abdulwasiu's own NeoLife network marketing team. Not a paying client.
- Owner: Abdulwasiu (team leader and portal admin)
- Country and city: Nigeria, Lagos
- Source: internal project
- Style and personality: wants many questions asked before building, then fast action

## Goal
A portal that tracks the team. In his words:
1. Know the total team, and who has a downline and who does not.
2. Track earnings. Members set a monthly goal and should meet it.
3. Every member has their own dashboard, sees how much they made, and can request a breakdown. The leader gets a notification.
4. Members save payment details so the breakdown is sent there directly.
5. A calculator: what a member earns if they do 250 PV, and if their team also does their points. Needs the products with PV and BV.

## Answers he gave (12 Nov 2026)
- Logins: every member logs in. Abdulwasiu is the only admin.
- Downline: each member has a sponsor. Picking the sponsor builds the tree.
- Earnings: members type them in with a screenshot as proof. Admin approves.
- Currency: Naira.
- Goals: members set a monthly goal. The portal breaks it into weeks and days, and suggests daily tasks they can add to a to do list.
- Device: phone browser and web app. No store app for now.
- Team size: 9 members now, target 30 in one year.
- Company: NeoLife. No API. He has a back office for resources only.
- Later: might sell the portal to other team leaders. For now only his team.

## Real problem
He cannot see at a glance who is working and who is not. Members without a clear daily target drift. Every member who stays active and sponsors others raises his own bonus level, so tracking is direct income for him.

## Budget and deadline
- Budget: none stated. Hosting should stay free or near free.

- Deadline: none stated. First version built 12 Nov 2026.
- Language: English
