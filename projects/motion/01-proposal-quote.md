# Proposal and Quote: Motion (personal project)

Currency: none. This is Abdulwasiu's own tool, so there is no client quote.

## Scope of version 1 (built)
Included:
- Logins for the leader (admin) and every member, with private invite links
- Team tree by sponsor, counts of who has a downline and who does not
- Monthly goals broken into weeks and days, with a pace bar
- Daily to do list with task ideas from the goal
- Earnings reports with screenshot proof, approve or reject with a reason
- Breakdown requests, with the member's bank details shown to the leader
- NeoLife Sales Volume Bonus calculator
- Product price list (PV, BV, prices), with paste from a spreadsheet
- Incentives page for the three NeoLife Nigeria events
- Leaderboard that shows progress, not Naira amounts

Not included in version 1:
- WhatsApp or email alerts (alerts show inside the portal)
- Play Store or App Store app
- Link to the NeoLife back office (NeoLife gives no API)
- More than one team in the same portal
