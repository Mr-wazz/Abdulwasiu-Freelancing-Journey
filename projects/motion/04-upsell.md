# Upsells: Motion (personal project)

There is no client to upsell. This file lists how the portal itself can earn money, in the order from Rule 3 for apps: money features, then store publishing, then promotion.

## Offers

### 1. Sell the portal to other team leaders as a subscription
- Problem it solves: every network marketing leader has the same problem. They cannot see who in the team is active, who has a downline and who is behind on goals.
- Money or time saved: a leader who keeps 3 more people active each month moves up a bonus level. On the NeoLife chart, going from 1,000 to 2,000 QPV lifts the bonus from 10% to 15% on the whole team volume.
- Price: 15 USD a month for up to 30 members, 39 USD a month for up to 100. First month free. Untested.
- Why this price: tools for single recruiters sell for about 5 to 7 USD a month. Company wide MLM software starts near 995 USD a month. A leader's team portal sits in the empty space between them.
- What must be built first: more than one team in one portal (each leader sees only their own team), and a sign up page for leaders.
- Message to send:
  "Hi [Name], I built a small portal for my NeoLife team. I can see everyone's goal and who is behind, and they report earnings with a screenshot. It lifted my team's activity. Want me to set one up for your team? First month is free."

### 2. Setup service for leaders who do not want to do it themselves
- Problem it solves: most leaders are not technical. Adding 30 members and a price list feels like work.
- Money or time saved: saves them a weekend.
- Price: 100 USD one time. Includes loading their team, their price list and a 30 minute training call.
- Message to send:
  "I can load your whole team and price list for you and train you in 30 minutes. You only send me a list of names and sponsors."

### 3. WhatsApp alerts add on
- Problem it solves: members forget to open the portal. A WhatsApp reminder brings them back.
- Money or time saved: more daily activity, more reported sales.
- Price: 10 USD a month extra per team, plus the message cost.
- What must be built first: a WhatsApp Business sender and a daily reminder job.
- Message to send:
  "Your members can get a WhatsApp reminder each morning with their target for the day. Want me to switch it on for your team?"

### 4. Store app (Play Store, then App Store)
- Problem it solves: an app icon on the phone feels more real than a web link, and it allows push notifications.
- Price: part of a Premium plan, or 500 USD one time per leader brand.
- Do this only after 5 paying leaders. The web app can be added to the home screen today.
