# NivLux Skin & Laser: Partner Cold Call Script

Who you call: Sacramento businesses from the lead list (gyms, studios, salons, nails, lashes, spas, barbers, bridal, tattoo, dance).
Goal of every call: NOT to sell on the phone. Goal = book a short visit or a 10 minute call with the owner.
Items marked [CONFIRM] must be approved by NivLux before you say them.

---

## Before you dial (2 minutes)

1. Open the lead in the Excel file. Read the "Why they fit" column and the business name.
2. Check the time. Sacramento is 9 hours behind Lagos (Pacific time).
   Best window: 10am to 4pm Sacramento = 7pm to 1am Lagos.
3. Use a US caller ID if possible (a 916 number from your dialer or Google Voice). Local numbers get answered more.
4. Smile when you talk. Speak slowly and clearly. Short sentences.
5. Have ready: pen, the Excel tracker, and the follow up text below.

---

## The call (about 2 minutes)

### Step 1: Opener (10 seconds)
> "Hi, good morning! My name is [Your Name], I'm calling on behalf of NivLux Skin & Laser, a nurse led laser and skin clinic on Scripps Drive, near Arden Fair. Quick question: am I speaking with the owner, or the person who handles partnerships?"

- **Owner:** go to Step 2.
- **Not the owner:** go to "Gatekeeper" below.

### Step 2: Permission (5 seconds)
> "Great. I'll be quick, I promise. Do you have 30 seconds?"

(If they say "I'm busy": "No problem, when is a better time today or tomorrow?" Write it down, hang up politely.)

### Step 3: Why I'm calling (20 seconds)
> "We're choosing a few local [gyms / salons / studios / bridal shops] in [their city] to offer an exclusive perk to their clients. It costs you nothing and takes no work on your side. Your clients get a special package from us, and you and your team get treated too."

### Step 4: Their hook (use the line for their business type)

| Business type | What to say |
|---|---|
| Gym, CrossFit, boxing | "Your members train hard to look good. Smooth skin helps them show it off. We have a men's package for back, shoulders and chest, and a women's package too." |
| Pilates, yoga, barre | "Your clients wear fitted, sleeveless gear and invest in their body. This is a premium perk that makes your membership feel even more special." |
| Hair salon | "Your clients already trust you with how they look. This gives you something extra to offer them, at no cost." |
| Nail salon | "Your pedicure and manicure clients care about smooth legs and arms. One small flyer on your counter is all it takes." |
| Lash, brow, threading | "Your clients come every few weeks for beauty upkeep. We can send our facial clients your way too, so it works both ways." |
| Day spa, massage | "Your clients love self care but you don't offer laser. Partnering keeps them happy instead of going to a competitor." |
| Barbershop | "A lot of men want to get rid of back and chest hair but don't know where to go. Your clients trust you, and we have a package made for men." |
| Bridal shop, wedding | "Brides start laser 6 to 12 months before the wedding. Adding a 'smooth by the big day' perk makes your shop look even more premium." |
| Tattoo studio | "Your clients shave before every tattoo. Laser makes that easier, and we also do tattoo removal by consultation, so we can refer clients both ways." |
| Dance or pole studio | "Your dancers perform in costumes, so smooth skin matters. This is a perk for your students and instructors." |

### Step 5: The offer (30 seconds)
> "Here's what your clients get:
> - **For women:** Brazilian, underarms, full legs and full arms. 10 sessions for each area for **$2,999**. Bought one by one, that's about $6,750.
> - **For men:** full back, shoulders and chest. 10 sessions each for **$2,299**. About $4,950 normally.
> - **Plus a free Hydra Diamond Facial**, a $199 value.
>
> And for you: a free facial for you and your team [CONFIRM], co branded flyers with a QR code for your counter, and a shout out on our Instagram. Zero cost to you."

### Step 6: The close (ask for the meeting)
> "The easiest next step: I'll have the team drop off flyers, and you come try the facial yourself so you know what your clients will get. Would **Tuesday or Thursday** work better for you?"

- **Yes:** get their name, best phone, email, and day and time. Repeat it back. Mark "Meeting booked" in Excel.
- **Maybe:** "No problem. Can I text you the details so you have them? What's the best number?" Mark "Interested: send info".

### Step 7: Thank them (always)
> "Thank you so much, [Name]. Have a beautiful day!"

## Gatekeeper (receptionist or staff)

> "No problem at all. What's the owner's name, and when is the best time to catch them?"

If they ask what it's about:
> "It's a free client perk partnership with NivLux Skin & Laser. No cost to the business. Could I leave my number for them, or is there a better time to call back?"

Write the owner's name in "Decision maker name", set a "Follow up date", mark "Gatekeeper: get owner".

## Objections and what to say

**"Not interested."**
> "Totally fair, most owners say that before they hear it costs nothing. Can I just text you a one page summary so you have it? No pressure."

**"We already partner with someone."**
> "That's great, it means your clients care about this. Many businesses work with 2 or 3 partners. Ours includes 10 sessions per area plus a free facial, which most clinics don't offer. Worth a quick look?"

**"Just send me an email."**
> "Happy to. What's the best email? And so I send the right thing, would this be more for your clients or your staff?"

**"What's in it for me? Do I get paid?"**
> "We keep it simple: perks for your clients and your team, plus free marketing for your business on our side. Our medical team sets the rules on referral rewards, so I'll ask them what more we can offer you."
(Never promise cash per client. California has rules on paying for patient referrals.)

**"I'm too busy."**
> "I understand, I'll be quick next time. Is morning or afternoon better this week?"

**"Is this a sales call?"**
> "It's a partnership call, not selling you anything. It's free for your business. It just gives your clients a special offer."

**"Where are you calling from? You don't sound local."** (If asked)
> "I'm part of NivLux's outreach team. The clinic is at 2 Scripps Drive in Sacramento, and you can see everything at nivluxskinlaser.com."
(Always be honest. Never say you are standing in the clinic.)

**"Can you lower the price for my clients?"**
> "Good question. I'll check with the clinic what extra we can do for your clients specifically and get back to you." [CONFIRM before promising anything]

## Rules

1. Call business numbers only, 9am to 6pm Sacramento time, Monday to Friday.
2. If anyone says "don't call again", say "Of course, sorry to bother you", and mark "Do not call". Never call them again.
3. Be honest: you call on behalf of NivLux. Never pretend to be a nurse or to be at the clinic.
4. No medical promises. Always say "results vary" and "the nurse decides at the free consultation".
5. Never promise cash for referrals, or discounts beyond the posted price, without NivLux's approval.
6. Fill in the Excel tracker right after every call. Not later.
