# Upsells: QuoteFlash (invoicing app)

Timing: NOW, the app just went live and Kujtim is happy and still working with us. Frame: "Here is what will help you earn more".
Payment: Kujtim pays on another platform (not Fiverr). No escrow, so every add on needs written scope and 50 percent upfront.
Prices are suggestions. Adjust once the brief is complete.

## Money features first

### 1. Pro plan with Apple In App Purchase (subscription)
- Problem it solves: the app is live but earns nothing from its own users. Invoicing is a daily work tool, so people pay monthly for it
- Money or time saved: recurring income from every freelancer or small business that uses it. Free plan limited to a few invoices a month, Pro unlimited, with logo, templates and reminders
- Suggested price: 1,200 to 1,500 USD
- Note: selling Pro inside the app must use Apple In App Purchase with Restore Purchases (Apple 3.1.1). Do not use Stripe for it
- Message: "Kujtim, congratulations, QuoteFlash is live! The next step to make it earn is a Pro plan: free users get a few invoices a month, Pro gets unlimited invoices, logo and templates. Payment goes through Apple, users upgrade in one tap and you collect monthly income. Want me to send a simple plan?"
- Status: not offered

### 2. Get invoices paid faster: payment links, automatic reminders, recurring invoices
- Problem it solves: users send invoices and then wait. Late payment is the number one pain of anyone who invoices
- Money or time saved: users get paid sooner, so they keep the app and tell others. Reminders and recurring invoices run by themselves
- Suggested price: 700 to 1,000 USD
- Note: invoice payment links (Stripe or Paystack) are for real world services, which Apple allows. Push or local reminders for overdue invoices need the notification checks from the App Store checklist
- Message: "Kujtim, the biggest reason people stay with an invoicing app is that it gets them paid faster. I can add a pay now link on every invoice, automatic reminders for overdue ones, and repeat invoices for monthly clients. Shall I send you the outline?"
- Status: not offered

## Store publishing

### 3. App Store optimization (ASO)
- Problem it solves: people search "invoice maker", "invoice generator", "receipt". The listing must match those words, with screenshots that show the invoice result
- Money or time saved: more free downloads with no ad spend
- Suggested price: 350 to 500 USD
- Message: "Kujtim, QuoteFlash is live, and the biggest free growth lever now is the App Store listing: keywords like invoice maker, screenshots and description. I can rewrite them so more people find and install it. Want me to review your listing and tell you what to fix?"
- Status: not offered

## Promotion

### 4. 30 day launch plan, landing page, review collection
- Problem it solves: a live app with no users and no ratings. The App Store ranks apps on installs and reviews
- Money or time saved: first 50 users, first 5 star reviews, and a repeatable way to get more. Target audience: freelancers, tradespeople, small shops
- Suggested price: 600 to 900 USD
- Message: "Kujtim, an app needs its first 50 users and first reviews fast. I can prepare a 30 day launch plan, a simple landing page, and a rating prompt inside the app, aimed at freelancers and small businesses who send invoices. Shall I send the outline?"
- Status: not offered

## Retain

### 5. Monthly care plan
- Problem it solves: iOS updates and Apple rule changes can break the app or get an update rejected
- Money or time saved: fixes, small updates and rejection handling without a new quote each time
- Suggested price: 150 USD per month
- Message: "To protect QuoteFlash after every Apple update, I can offer a simple monthly care plan: fixes, updates and help if Apple rejects something. Want me to explain?"
- Status: not offered

## Other ideas (later)
- Android and Play Store version (invoicing users are on both)
- GHL follow up for users who stop sending invoices
- Analytics dashboard: invoices sent, paid, overdue
- Referral: ask Kujtim for another app owner after he is happy

## Best order to sell
Offer 1 now (it pays for itself), then 2 once Pro is live, then 3 and 4 together as a growth package.
